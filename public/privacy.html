⌁ LUMEN

PRIVACY SUMMARY

Typing stays private by default.

Lumen stores keyboard preferences and learned words locally on the device. It does not sell typed text or use it for advertising.

Dictionary and suggestions work locally. Grammar, Rewrite and Translate are optional. Before an AI request, Lumen shows the selected text that will be sent to the configured secure service. The service is designed not to log that text. Never paste passwords, account numbers, security codes or other sensitive information into an AI request.

Lumen disables personal learning, suggestions and AI tools in password and sensitive fields. You can clear learned words, clipboard entries and the AI access token in the app settings.

This summary will be replaced with the final public privacy policy before a Play Store release.
